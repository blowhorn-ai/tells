Remote-control tells

Group: automation. Since master. Catalog 1.0.1.

What Tells reads

  • Known automation globals on window and driver keys on document
  • A CDP serialization probe (a known side channel of the DevTools protocol)
  • Whether the webdriver getter is native, and whether the property was redefined to hide
  • Whether everyday native functions (Function.prototype.toString, permissions.query) have been replaced by script
  • Whether Function.prototype.toString and the navigator.webdriver, languages, and plugins getters agree across the page, a same-origin iframe, and a Web Worker
  • Input event trust: scripted events anywhere on the page, the interaction test's typing and clicks included, and events while the document is hidden or unfocused
  • Impossible window geometry (a zero outer size)

How the result is decided

  • Pass: No machinery found and no patch seams showing.
  • Warning: The CDP probe tripped, everyday functions were replaced by script, Function.prototype.toString or a navigator getter disagrees across contexts, or trusted input arrived while the page lacked focus.
  • Fail: Driver globals or document keys found, scripted input, input into a hidden page, a zero-size window, or evidence the webdriver flag was hidden by patching. These are the most specific findings in the whole report.
  • Not available: Remote-control readings were not collected in this run.

A patch that looks the same in every context keeps its existing reason and points.
