Behavioural classification

Group: behavior. Since v0.3.3. Catalog 1.0.3.

What Tells reads

  • Pointer, keyboard, scroll, focus and visibility events and their timing during the visit
  • The activity from the interaction test, when taken

How the result is decided

  • Pass: A score of 0.5 or better. A short, still visit scores lower than an active one; that is the measure working, not an accusation.
  • Warning: A score from 0.25 to 0.5: borderline texture. A run with no input observed also warns, as no input, not as automated input.
  • Fail: A behaviour score under 0.25 on input that was actually observed, or evidence that is missing or does not hold together.
  • Not available: Behaviour evidence was not collected in this run.

Findings

  • behavior_score_low: high, 25 points. since v0.2.0
  • behavior_score_borderline: medium, 12 points. since v0.2.0
  • behavior_no_input: low, 0 points. since master
  • behavior_evidence_invalid: high, 25 points. since v0.3.3

Note

The row shipped in v0.2.0. behavior_evidence_invalid was added in v0.3.3 when the Worker started deriving the behaviour score (#35, #38). behavior_no_input was added in master for #83: a run over 3.5s with no input observed is reported as no input, not as automated input, and costs no points.
